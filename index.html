01 / The idea

Geometry is not the whole visual state.

Geometry describes structure; the future frame also needs appearance. UPCAST learns the two roles in a predictive transition and transfers their benefit into a standalone video model.

01 / THE OBSERVATION

One video, complementary evidence.

A geometric representation can preserve layout and correspondence, but it cannot alone specify the scene's color, texture, and lighting.

03 / How it works

Learn together. Generate alone.

The physical and appearance states are training-time representations. Step through how they are learned, transferred, and removed from the inference path.

PAIRWISE TRAINING

Separate what stays shared from what remains visual.

TRAINING-ONLY TEACHER

Move useful corrections into the video backbone.
