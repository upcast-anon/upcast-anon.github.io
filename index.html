01 / The idea

Geometry is not the whole visual state.

Geometry describes structure; the future frame also needs appearance. UPCAST learns the two roles in a predictive transition and transfers their benefit into a standalone video model.

03 / How it works

Learn together. Generate alone.

The physical and appearance states are training-time representations. Their predictive benefit is transferred into the original video backbone.

CHECKPOINT LINEAGE

Follow the handoff.

Unified-space learning improves FVD; the backbone-only handoff retains most of that gain.

Representations carry different predictive information.

On 192 held-out transitions, physical video-to-geometry retrieval is 13.54% Top-1 versus 0.52% chance. Private-state retrieval is 70.31% Top-1; removing private input raises frozen visual-decoder loss by 45.1%.

The transfer extends to action control.

On 100 matched Minecraft quickstart videos, Action-DFoT + UPCAST improves FVD from 245.0 to 221.7, PSNR from 15.83 to 15.97, and paired action response from 86.89% to 89.78%.
